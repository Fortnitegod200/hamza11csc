About Us

At Climate Change NZ, our goal is to make climate information clear, accessible, and relevant for everyone in Aotearoa. We provide trusted resources, practical guidance, and up-to-date information to help individuals, communities, businesses, and organisations better understand climate change and respond with confidence. We believe that informed decisions lead to meaningful action, whether that's reducing emissions, preparing for climate impacts, or supporting sustainable initiatives. By bringing together reliable information in one place, we aim to empower New Zealanders to take positive steps toward a more resilient, low-emissions future and help protect the people, places, and environment that make Aotearoa unique.
